Since early May, researchers at the National Institute of Biomedical Research (INRB) and their partners have been sequencing isolates from the ongoing Bundibugyo ebolavirus outbreak in the DRC.

Together with the INRB, we are maintaining an outbreak-focused tree with the latest sequencing data, and have prepared a detailed analysis of the current data in the form of a Nextstrain narrative.

[https://nextstrain.org/narratives/ebola/bdbv/drc-uganda-2026/2026-09-01](https://nextstrain.org/narratives/ebola/bdbv/drc-uganda-2026/2026-09-01)
